# Presentation and appendix examples

## Replace a generic agenda with a message path

**Weak**

1. 背景
2. 課題
3. 施策
4. 今後

**Stronger**

1. 分析人材不足だけでは、実装件数の停滞を説明できない
2. 滞留は分析工程より、案件受付と実装責任の確定で発生している
3. 来期は増員より、受付・責任・効果測定の標準化を優先する
4. 3部署・8週間の試行で、全社展開の可否を判断できる

## One slide, one message

**Weak title**

> 現状の課題

**Weak body**

- 人材不足
- データ品質
- 権限
- 文化
- ガバナンス

**Stronger title**

> 案件の停滞は分析人材ではなく、要件と実装責任者が未確定の工程に集中している

**Stronger body**

Use one flow chart or stage-duration chart that proves where the delay occurs. Move secondary issues to appendix or later slides.

## Core versus appendix

Candidate: department-by-department breakdown of all 18 metrics.

- Core: one chart showing the two metrics that determine the recommendation.
- Appendix A3–A6: department-level detail for likely questions.
- Omit: duplicate tables that do not change interpretation.

Candidate: assumption that projected savings require 70% adoption.

- Core: keep the assumption and sensitivity because it can change the business case.
- Appendix: detailed adoption model and segment scenarios.

## Speaker notes versus slide

**Slide**

> 権限申請の標準化だけで、分析着手までの中央値を短縮できる

**Speaker note**

> ここではシステム改修ではなく、申請項目と承認責任者の統一を指しています。実装例はA2にあります。

Do not put the only definition of “standardization” in the note if the audience could misread the slide.

## Appendix title

**Weak**

> Appendix 3：詳細データ

**Stronger**

> A3｜改善は全体平均だけでなく、対象3部署すべてで確認できる

Trigger question: “一部部署だけの改善ではないか？”

## Cognitive rhythm

**Flat sequence**

- explanation
- explanation
- explanation
- explanation

**Stronger sequence**

- Orient: exact ask and recommendation
- Tension: staffing does not explain the bottleneck
- Observe: stage-duration evidence
- Reframe: the constraint is intake and ownership
- Compare: staffing-first versus process-first
- Decide: approve the 8-week pilot
- Act: owners, measures, and checkpoint

## Visual variety with a reason

Do not replace every slide with a different template. Use the same comparison layout across options, but change to a causal diagram when the audience must understand mechanism and to a roadmap when they must understand sequence.
