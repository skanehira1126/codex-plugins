# Presentation patterns by audience task

Choose by what the audience must do. Omit anything that does not earn presentation time.

## Decision or executive approval deck

Core sequence:

1. Decision requested and recommendation
2. Why the decision is needed now
3. Decisive diagnosis and evidence
4. Options and evaluation criteria
5. Recommended choice and why alternatives lose
6. Material risks, reversibility, and mitigation
7. Owner, timing, measures, and explicit ask

Typical appendix:

- detailed data and definitions;
- sensitivity or scenario analysis;
- rejected options;
- implementation detail;
- methodology and source traceability.

## Proposal deck

Core sequence:

1. Current outcome or friction the audience recognizes
2. Why the obvious explanation is insufficient
3. Root cause or binding constraint
4. Design principles for a valid response
5. Proposed intervention and mechanism
6. Evidence, tradeoffs, and boundaries
7. Pilot, decision checkpoint, and ask

Typical appendix:

- detailed solution architecture;
- alternative vendors or designs;
- cost model;
- rollout workstreams;
- extended case evidence.

## Analytical findings deck

Core sequence:

1. Question and headline finding
2. Scope and minimum method needed to interpret the result
3. Most decision-relevant observation
4. Explanation or alternative interpretation
5. Consequence for the target decision
6. Remaining uncertainty and next evidence

Typical appendix:

- full methodology;
- sample and variable definitions;
- robustness checks;
- segment cuts;
- supplementary charts.

## Strategy deck

Core sequence:

1. Changed condition and strategic challenge
2. Binding constraint or decisive diagnosis
3. Explicit strategic choice
4. What the organization will not do
5. Required capabilities and operating changes
6. Sequenced initiatives and dependencies
7. metrics, review triggers, and ask

Typical appendix:

- market or competitor detail;
- initiative charters;
- financial model;
- capability assessment;
- risk scenarios.

## Training or explanatory deck

Core sequence:

1. Familiar situation or practical problem
2. Simple map or model
3. Concrete example
4. Mechanism and concept
5. Guided application
6. Misconception or failure mode
7. Reconstruction and next use

Typical appendix:

- glossary;
- additional exercises;
- detailed examples;
- reference tables;
- facilitator notes.

## Status or intervention deck

Core sequence:

1. Objective and current status
2. Material change since prior checkpoint
3. Evidence and milestone movement
4. Risks, blockers, and ownership
5. Decision or support required
6. Next checkpoint

Typical appendix:

- workstream detail;
- issue log;
- milestone definitions;
- historical trend;
- resource breakdown.
