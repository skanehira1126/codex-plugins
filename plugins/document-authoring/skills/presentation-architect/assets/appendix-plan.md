## Appendix gates

- [ ] Every appendix slide answers a plausible question or verification need.
- [ ] No appendix slide exists only because the source contained the material.
- [ ] Titles communicate an answer, not merely “参考” or “Appendix.”
- [ ] Each slide is independently understandable and sourced.
- [ ] No conclusion-changing risk, limitation, or missing evidence is hidden here.
- [ ] Frequently needed material has been promoted to the core or summarized there.
- [ ] Navigation and stable IDs support fast Q&A retrieval.
